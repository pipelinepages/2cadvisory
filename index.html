Founder & Principal
"Founders don't need more legal jargon. They need someone who tells them clearly what protects them and what doesn't."
Comforter Bolere Ketebu (CBK) — Founder & Principal, 2C Legal Advisory

Comforter Bolere Ketebu, Esq. is the Founder and Principal Counsel of 2C Legal Advisory (2CLA), a corporate, commercial, technology, and startup-focused law practice that helps founders, SMEs, and growth-stage businesses build legally sound and investment-ready companies.

With experience advising startups, business owners, and emerging ventures across corporate governance, fundraising, commercial transactions, regulatory compliance, data protection, and technology law, Comforter is passionate about helping entrepreneurs identify legal risks before they become business problems.

Beyond her legal practice, she is committed to making quality legal guidance more accessible to founders through legal education and founder-focused content. She is the creator of the Startup Blueprint Series, a YouTube platform where she shares practical legal and business insights to help entrepreneurs build sustainable, compliant, and scalable businesses. She is also exploring innovative ways to leverage AI to help founders identify legal gaps early and access affordable legal support.

About 2C Legal

Built for founders who move fast — and still need to get it right.

2C Legal was started because too many founders were learning legal lessons the expensive way: after a bad contract, a missed compliance step, or a dispute over who actually owns the code. We work as an embedded legal partner for startups and technology companies, translating dense legal requirements into clear decisions you can act on.

Our team combines traditional corporate and commercial law with deep familiarity in technology, data, and intellectual property — including trademark and patent protection, and the software licensing agreements that determine how your product can be sold, white-labeled, or integrated by others. We pair that expertise with our AI Legal Assessment tool, so every engagement starts with a clear picture of where you stand.
